Send researched independent businesses to Kanso's Genesis agent.

Base URL: https://app.trykanso.co
Endpoint: POST /api/genesis/intake
Schema: /genesis-intake-openapi.json

Use the Genesis token supplied privately by the Kanso administrator:
Authorization: Bearer YOUR_GENESIS_TOKEN
Content-Type: application/json
Idempotency-Key: a unique stable identifier for this submission (8–200 characters)

Send one JSON object per request. Example (fictional data):
{
  "business_name": "Example Coffee",
  "owner_name": "Jamie Morgan",
  "email": "jamie@example.com",
  "phone": "+1 312 555 0100",
  "website": "https://example.com",
  "city": "Chicago, IL",
  "industry": "Food & Beverage",
  "is_franchise": false,
  "research_notes": "Include evidence, source URLs, and why the business could expand.",
  "franchise_information": "Describe the concept and franchise potential."
}

Only submit businesses already researched and confirmed not to be franchises.
Find the business owner's full name when possible. Never invent contact information.
business_name is required. Also include at least one of email, website, owner_name,
phone, or city. Include website and city whenever available to reduce ambiguity.

If you have an email, include it. Genesis skips discovery and only verifies the email.
If email is missing, omit the field (or send null). Genesis runs owner discovery and
email enrichment, preserving the name, phone, and research you supplied.
Do not submit placeholders such as "unknown" as an email.
An invalid email format returns 400; correct it or omit it, then use a new request key.

A 202 response means the lead is saved and queued, not that the email is verified.
Keep the returned id. Poll GET /api/genesis/intake?id=RECEIPT_ID with the same
Authorization header about once per minute until status is complete or failed.
Processing usually spans several queue ticks. Do not resubmit just because it is slow.

After a timeout or network error, repeat exactly the same payload and Idempotency-Key.
An identical retry returns the existing receipt. A changed payload with the same key
returns 409. A corrected submission needs a new key.

Outcomes:
- ready_for_review: email passed verification; waits in Genesis Incoming Leads.
- held_catch_all: email domain accepts arbitrary recipients; held for review.
- held_invalid_email: supplied email failed verification; held for correction.
- held_unverified: verification was unavailable or inconclusive; held for review.
- no_email: discovery/enrichment finished without finding an email.
- loaded: a Kanso user loaded the prospect into the existing outreach campaign.
- skipped_duplicate: the contact already exists in the campaign system.

Genesis skips scouting and franchise screening for these prequalified submissions.
Submission itself does not send email. A Kanso user controls campaign loading.
Keep the bearer token out of URLs, research notes, and public documents.
